# OpenVitals 2.11.1

Released 2026-09-28.

This release makes OpenVitals read right in every language. The steps ring on the dashboard no longer says "steps" in English, heart rate shows the unit each language uses, counts of days, nights and tests agree with the number, and the health disclaimer matches the English again in Czech, Polish, Russian and Estonian. The Google Play listing now speaks all 14 app languages, with a new description and eight screenshots in each.

### Changed

- **Google Play in every app language.** The store listing has a new description and eight new screenshots in each of the 14 languages the app speaks. Nine of them are new on Google Play: Czech, Finnish, French, Galician, Japanese, Polish, Portuguese, Russian and Simplified Chinese.
- **Heart rate in your language's unit.** Heart rate shows the unit your language uses where it has its own: lpm in Spanish, ppm in Galician, tep/min in Czech and уд/мин in Russian, on the charts, the cards, the workout recording screen and the body profile. Czech says "tep/min" throughout, which also fits the heart rate chart axis.

### Fixed

- **The steps ring in your language.** The dashboard's steps ring read "steps of 8,000" in English in every language.
- **"1 day", not "1 days".** Days, nights and tests take the right form for the number in every language, on the statistics cards, the dashboard and the home-screen widgets.
- **The health disclaimer.** Czech and Russian left out "cure", Polish added "mitigate", and Estonian had a grammar error. All four match the English again, on the onboarding screen, in Settings and in the privacy text.

The app keeps the same package name and signing certificate, so this installs as a normal update.

Distribution flow:

- Codeberg release with signed APK, signed debug APK, and signed Android App Bundle assets.
- Direct Google Play production upload from the approved Woodpecker deployment.
