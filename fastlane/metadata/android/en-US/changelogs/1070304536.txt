One Body composition form in Add entry saves everything a body analysis scale shows (weight, body fat, lean mass, body water, bone mass, BMR) at one time. Blood glucose and VO2 max can be entered and edited. Every kind of medical record Health Connect holds can be typed in; Medical records are marked beta, with a one-time notice. A Wear OS watch's steps also give cadence and walking calories. The sleep home-screen widget shows time asleep, as the Summary does.
